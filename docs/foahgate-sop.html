How it works in one minute

One shipment is one record from the moment a business unit asks for a truck until that truck leaves the gate. Four roles touch it, each at a different stage. The chain below is the order things happen.

The one rule everyone should know

A truck is only admitted against a valid gate pass. No pass, no entry. Security can still record an exception, but the warehouse admin decides what happens next.

Section 3

Signing in

  1. Open the system. Go to foahgate.vercel.app. On a phone, add it to the home screen for one tap access.
  2. Pick your role. Admin, Customer, Carrier or Security. The role must match your account or you get a clear error telling you which role your account has.
  3. Enter email and password. Accounts are created by the warehouse admin. There is no self registration and no password reset link, ask the admin.
  4. You land on your home screen. Sessions last 12 hours. Sign out from your name at the top right.
Shared devices

The gate house tablet stays signed in as the Security account for the shift. Sign out at shift end. Never use an admin account on the gate tablet.

Section 4

Customer procedure

Al Foah Dates, Grand Mills
Who
Supply and logistics planners in the Agthia business unit whose cargo it is.
Goal
Tell the warehouse what is coming or going, get a carrier booked, and follow it to gate out.
Sees
Only the shipments of their own business unit.

4.1 Raise a shipment request

  1. Overview, New shipment request.
  2. Direction. Inbound is a delivery to the warehouse (unloading). Outbound is a dispatch (loading).
  3. Expected date, priority, cargo type. The cargo type sets how long the truck will hold a dock. Containers reserve two slots. Leave the handling override empty unless the warehouse told you a different time.
  4. Cargo description, quantity and unit. Write what the dock team will see: "Khalas dates, field bins, 42 bins".
  5. References. Inbound: BL number, PO number. Outbound: invoice number. Container and seal if it is containerised. These print on the gate pass.
  6. Carrier. Choose the transport company now, or leave "Assign later". Extra fields under Additional details are set by the warehouse and may be required.
  7. Raise shipment request. The reference number appears, for example ALF-INB-2026-00142. Quote it in every conversation about this load.

4.2 Assign a carrier

  1. Open the shipment, Assign carrier. Requests without a carrier also appear under Needs your action on the overview.
  2. Pick the carrier and confirm. The carrier gets an in-app notification and a booking link. Copy booking link on the shipment page gives you the same link to send by WhatsApp or email.
  3. Wait for the carrier. Status changes from Awaiting truck details to Booked once they have picked a slot. You can change the carrier until then.

4.3 Track, edit and cancel

  • Overview shows today's movements, the next seven days, and anything waiting on you.
  • The shipment page shows the lifecycle stepper, the booked truck and dock, every gate event with its time, and the full history.
  • Edit details is available until the booking is confirmed. After that, ask the warehouse admin.
  • Cancel request is available until the truck arrives. Give a reason, the carrier is notified and the slot is released.
Amber notice on your overview

"Rescheduled by the system" means an early truck was put into the dock your booking was holding, and your booking was moved to the next free slot automatically. Read the new time, then press Acknowledge. Nothing else is needed from you.

Section 5

Carrier procedure

Transport companies and Agthia Fleet
Who
Dispatch staff at the transport company.
Goal
Put a truck and driver against each assigned shipment, choose a slot, and hand the driver a gate pass.
Sees
Only shipments assigned to their company.

5.1 Book a truck against a shipment

  1. Overview, Needs truck details. Or open the booking link the customer sent. Sign in as Carrier if asked.
  2. Check the shipment summary. Customer, direction, cargo, expected date and the dock time it will reserve.
  3. Truck. Truck plate exactly as on the vehicle, trailer plate if any, truck type, capacity.
  4. Driver. Name, mobile, Emirates ID or licence number. Security checks these against the driver at the gate.
  5. Pick a slot. The grid shows one column per dock that can take this direction and one row per time. Green is free. Grey slots show why they are not available when you hover. Use the arrows to change the day, up to 14 days ahead. Slots less than 60 minutes away cannot be booked.
  6. Confirm booking. If someone took the slot a second earlier you get a message and the grid reloads, pick another. On success the gate pass appears, for example GP-2026-00142.

5.2 Give the driver the pass

  • Download PDF and print it, or send the PDF to the driver.
  • Open mobile pass gives a phone page with the QR code. Send that link to the driver, it works without signing in.
  • Gate passes lists every pass you have been issued. Reprint from there at any time.
  • The QR code holds a random token, not the reference number. A pass cannot be guessed or edited.

5.3 Cancel or change

  • Cancel booking is allowed until 2 hours before the slot. Give a reason. The customer is notified and the slot is freed for others.
  • To change the truck or the time after booking, cancel and book again, or ask the warehouse admin to move it.
  • If the warehouse enables approval mode, the pass shows Pending approval and is not valid at the gate until an admin approves it.
Tell your drivers

Arrive within the slot. A truck that arrives early may be admitted straight to a free dock, or it waits in the yard. A truck that has not arrived 60 minutes after the slot ends is marked as a no show, which counts in the carrier performance report.

Section 6

Security procedure

Gate House 1, phone or tablet
Who
The guard on duty at the gate.
Goal
Admit only valid trucks, send each one to the right place, record every movement with a time.
Sees
Scan screen, trucks inside now, trucks expected today.

6.1 A truck arrives

  1. Scan the QR code on the driver's printed pass or phone, using Scan QR with camera. Or type the gate pass number or shipment reference and press Look up.
  2. Read the banner.
GRANTED

Pass is valid for entry now. Compare the truck plate, the driver and the ID with the card, then press Gate in.

CHECK

Something is wrong and the reasons are listed: pass already used, booking cancelled, truck already inside, waiting for approval. Do not admit unless the warehouse tells you to. You can still Gate in with a written reason, or Flag exception.

  1. Press Gate in. The system decides where the truck goes and shows it in large text. Tell the driver.

Green result: Proceed to DOCK-03

The truck goes straight to that dock. If it arrived early, the screen also says it was promoted into a free dock. If its booked dock was busy, it says it was redirected.

Amber result: Wait in yard, position 2

No compatible dock is free. Direct the truck to the yard. It is called to a dock later by the warehouse, or by you with Call to first free dock.

6.2 While the truck is inside

  • Inside now lists every truck inside with its location and how long it has been in. Open any row to get back to its card.
  • Send to yard moves a truck off a dock or from the gate to the yard, for example when the dock is not ready.
  • Call to first free dock moves a yard truck to the first compatible dock that is free.
  • Expected lists today's booked trucks that have not arrived, with their slot time, so you know who is due.

6.3 A truck leaves

  1. Look up the pass again (scan, or open it from Inside now).
  2. Press Gate out. If handling is complete this is one tap. If handling is not complete, the system asks for a note explaining why the truck is leaving. Write it.

6.4 Something is wrong

  1. Press Flag exception. Choose the problem: wrong truck plate, documents missing, damaged seal, driver does not match, other.
  2. Write what you see. For example "Truck plate is AD 55 90121, pass shows AD 12345". The admin team is notified immediately.
  3. Hold the truck. The admin clears the exception and tells you whether to admit it, send it to the yard, or turn it away.
Never

Never admit a truck without pressing Gate in, and never let one leave without Gate out. Every timestamp in the reports comes from these two buttons.

Section 7

Admin procedure

Warehouse manager and control room
Who
Warehouse manager and operations supervisors.
Goal
Keep the docks moving, resolve exceptions, keep the setup correct, and read the numbers.
Sees
Everything, across all customers and carriers.

7.1 Daily routine

  1. Start of day, Overview. Booked today, still to arrive, exceptions, no shows. Needs attention lists approvals, exceptions, yard trucks and overdue arrivals with a link to each.
  2. Keep the Dock board open on the control room screen (the warehouse operations account lands there). It refreshes every 15 seconds. Each dock shows the truck on it, how long it has been there, and the next three bookings.
  3. At the dock: Start handling when work begins, Finish handling when done. The truck then shows Completed and security can gate it out.
  4. Yard board: Call to dock when a compatible dock frees up. Choose the dock or take the first free one.
  5. End of day, Run no show check on the overview. It also runs automatically every morning at 06:00.

7.2 Decisions on a booking

Open any booking from Bookings, the dock board, the yard, or a notification. The action bar shows only the actions that make sense for its current status.

  • Approve or Reject (approval mode only). Rejecting needs a reason and releases the slot.
  • Move to another slot. Pick a new dock and time on the grid and write the reason. Carrier and customer are notified with your reason.
  • Cancel booking. Any time before gate out, with a reason.
  • Clear exception. Decide where the truck continues from: Booked (turned away, may return), Arrived, In yard, or At dock. Write the decision note.
  • Gate out with note. For a truck that must leave before handling is complete.
  • The Audit trail at the bottom of the page shows every change, who made it, when, and what changed.

7.3 Slot planner

  • One day at a time, every dock, every slot. Booked cells link to the booking.
  • Block a slot with a reason (forklift maintenance, stock count). Carriers cannot book it. Unblock to reopen.
  • Taking a whole dock out of service is done under Docks, status Maintenance or Blocked. Bookings already on that dock must be moved from the bookings list.

7.4 Setup pages

Docks

Code, name, type (inbound, outbound, both), status, notes. Type decides which shipments a dock is offered for.

Cargo types

Each type carries handling minutes. This is how much dock time a booking reserves. Set these with the dock team.

Organisations and Users

Add carriers and business units, then their users. A user's role must match the organisation type. Disable users who leave, do not delete.

Custom fields

Add fields to the customer shipment form or the carrier booking form: text, number, date, dropdown, checkbox, document reference. They appear immediately and print on the pass.

Configuration

Operating hours, slot length, buffer, max concurrent trucks, early arrival rule, late tolerance, no show timer, approval mode, booking horizon, lead time, cancel cutoff, truck types, units, gate pass text.

Reports and Audit log

Trucks per day, turnaround, dock utilisation, on time versus late by carrier, no shows, exceptions, with CSV download. The audit log is filterable and cannot be edited.

Changing the slot grid

Changing operating hours, slot length or buffer regenerates all open future slots. Existing bookings keep their times. Do it outside operating hours and check the slot planner afterwards.

Section 9

Rules the system enforces

These are not guidelines, the software applies them. All the numbers come from the Configuration page and can be changed by an admin.

No double booking

Two carriers cannot book the same dock for overlapping time, even if they click at the same second. The database refuses the second one and the carrier is told to pick another slot.

Slots come from the grid

06:00 to 22:00, 60 minute slots, 15 minute buffer between them. A booking holds as many consecutive slots as its cargo type needs.

Early arrival rule

A truck gated in before its slot goes straight to a compatible dock that is free for its full handling time. If the only free dock is held by a later booking, that booking is moved to the next free slot and both parties are notified. If nothing is free, the truck waits in the yard.

Arrival timing

Trucks may arrive up to 4 hours early. Later than 30 minutes after slot start counts as late in reports. 60 minutes after the slot ends with no gate in becomes a no show.

Booking window

Carriers can book up to 14 days ahead and no closer than 60 minutes before a slot. Carriers cannot cancel within 2 hours of the slot.

Everything is logged

Every status change, move, cancellation, approval, configuration change and login is written to the audit log with the person and the exact time. It cannot be edited.

10.2 Go live checklist

  • Run the clean reset once. It removes all demo shipments, bookings, gate events and audit rows and keeps organisations, users, docks, cargo types, configuration and custom fields.
  • Change every seeded password from Admin, Users. Disable any demo account that is not needed.
  • Add the real carriers and business units under Organisations, then their users.
  • Confirm dock names, types and cargo handling minutes with the dock team.
  • Review Configuration with the warehouse manager: hours, slot length, early arrival rule, approval mode.
  • Set the gate tablet up with the Security account and the camera permission allowed.
  • Print this document and keep a copy at the gate house and the planning desk.
